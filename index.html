>_ PROJECTS

SELECTED WORK

Hope Harbor

The primary objective of Hope Harbor is to educate players on environmental restoration and resource management through an engaging simulation. Players are tasked with cleaning a polluted ocean harbor, transforming collected trash into valuable resources, and using those resources to rebuild a sustainable community. The game aims to create a positive feedback loop, showing how direct environmental action leads to tangible rewards — healing wildlife, growing food, and earning reputation.

ThisIsMe

ThisIsMe is an Android app designed to help users describe themselves. Whether you need a quick bio for a new social account, a polished intro for a class, or just want to put your story into words, ThisIsMe walks you through a short, focused flow to build a shareable personal profile — entirely on-device, no account required.

OmniSense

An Arduino-based multi-sensor rig that logs and visualizes environmental data. OmniSense combines several discrete sensors into one compact board, streams readings to a companion dashboard, and now feeds its data into Project Iris for unified environmental analytics.
